# Data retention

## Ledger entries
Ledger entries are kept for 10 years to meet financial record-keeping rules. They are never deleted, only archived to cold storage after 2 years.

## Application logs
Application logs are kept for 30 days in the log platform, then deleted.

## Card data
We never store full card numbers; the acquirer returns a token. Tokens are deleted 13 months after the last successful charge.

## Customer deletion requests
Personal data is deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request, except data we must keep for financial records, which is pseudonymised instead.
